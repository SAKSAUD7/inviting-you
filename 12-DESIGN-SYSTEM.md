# INVITING YOU — DESIGN SYSTEM PRINCIPLES

## PUBLIC BRAND

The public brand should feel:

quiet luxury
editorial
warm
trustworthy
modern
cultural

Do not force one color across every template.

The brand shell can be consistent while invitation designs are expressive.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Use typography as a major design asset.

Public site:

- elegant display serif
- clean sans

Invitation templates:

each template may have a unique pairing.

Avoid more than 2–3 primary typefaces in one invitation.

---

# MOTION

Use three levels:

Micro:
buttons, icons, small feedback.

Section:
scroll reveals.

Cinematic:
opening and major transitions.

Do not animate everything.

---

# SPACING

Luxury requires breathing room.

Prefer fewer elements with stronger hierarchy.

---

# IMAGERY

Images should feel:

editorial
intentional
high quality

Use:

- crop control
- art-directed aspect ratios
- overlays
- masks
- parallax
- light treatment

Do not use random grids by default.

---

# DECORATION

Decorations must support the story.

If the story is moonlit:

stars, moonlight, architecture.

If the story is Kerala:

jasmine, lamp, kasavu-inspired geometry.

If the story is editorial:

grain, typography, whitespace.

---

# CULTURAL DESIGN

Research cultural references carefully.

Avoid superficial stereotypes.

Do not mix religious/cultural symbols casually.

When a template represents a cultural tradition, the visual system should be coherent and respectful.

---

# TEMPLATE UNIQUENESS

Every template should answer:

What is the visual metaphor?

What is the opening ritual?

What is the signature interaction?

What makes this template recognizable in one second?

If the answer is unclear, the template is not ready.

---

# MOBILE-FIRST

Design the actual mobile composition first.

Desktop is an expansion.

---

# PERFORMANCE

Beauty must survive:

mid-range Android
mobile network
WhatsApp deep link
older browser

---

# ADMIN DESIGN

Admin is a tool.

Do not make it ornamental.

Use:

clear hierarchy
fast tables
filters
forms
validation
search
keyboard-friendly interactions
