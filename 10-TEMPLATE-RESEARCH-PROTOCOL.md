# RESEARCH QUESTIONS

For each relevant reference:

1. What emotion is it trying to create?
2. What is the visual metaphor?
3. What is the opening interaction?
4. What is the typography hierarchy?
5. What is the section rhythm?
6. How is imagery treated?
7. What cultural elements are used?
8. What is generic/overused?
9. What is the strongest idea?
10. What could be done differently?

---

# TRANSFORMATION METHOD

Never:

competitor
→ copy
→ modify colors
→ ship

Instead:

competitor
→ abstract principle
→ combine with another broad principle
→ create a new story
→ choose new composition
→ create original art direction
→ create original motion
→ implement independently

---

# DESIGN DIFFERENTIATION TEST

Before approval, compare the proposed design against references.

Ask:

Would a reasonable viewer confuse this with the source?

If yes:

change:

- composition
- typography
- color
- visual metaphor
- motion
- artwork
- section order

until the design is clearly independent.

---

# ASSET RULE

Use:

- original assets
- commissioned assets
- properly licensed assets
- generated assets with appropriate rights
